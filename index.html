Who we are

One region, one standard of care.

The directors who run EMS in each Upper Cumberland county don't do the job in isolation. The association is how they share what works — and speak with one voice when it counts.

A network of directors

EMS leadership from across Region 4, meeting to compare protocols, problems, and solutions.

Shared education

Continuing education and hands-on training that a single county could rarely stand up alone.

Mutual aid, coordinated

When a call outgrows one county, neighbors respond. The relationships are built here first.

Recognition & remembrance

We honor the region's best each year — and we don't forget those we've lost.

Region 4 honors

Recognizing the region's best.

Each year at the symposium, the association presents its Region 4 awards — nominated by directors, earned on the worst days of someone else's year.

EMT of the Year

For an emergency medical technician whose skill and character set the regional standard.

Paramedic of the Year

Honoring advanced-level care that goes above and beyond in the field.

For the crew whose response to a critical incident exemplified the best of EMS.
